FAQ

House cleaning website questions

Can you show weekly, biweekly and monthly pricing?

Yes. We set up your pricing the way you charge, by home size, frequency or both.

Can you link to my booking software?

Yes. If you already use an online booking tool, we can link the Book button to it. Otherwise the Get My Price form comes to you.

What should my guarantee say?

Whatever you actually offer, for example a free re-clean if something's missed. We help you word it clearly.

Should I show my team?

If you can, yes. Real photos of your team make people more comfortable letting you in their home.

Do you build for Airbnb or move-out cleaning businesses?

Yes. We add service cards for turnovers, move-in and move-out cleans, or whatever you offer.

How fast is it?

Your site is live 5 days after you send your logo, phone number, cities and photos. The clock starts when your assets arrive.

What's the free mockup?

Send your business name, trade and city, and we'll make a mockup of your homepage so you can see it before you pay anything. No cost, no obligation.

Are there monthly fees?

Only if you want Care. Monthly Care is $99/mo for hosting plus 2 small edits a month. Without Care, we hand the site off to your own host.

Who owns the site?

You do. You own your domain, your content and the final page files once paid in full.
